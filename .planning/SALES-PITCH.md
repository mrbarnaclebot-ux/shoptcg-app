# tcgmart.app: your card table, on every buyer's phone

Put a QR code on your table. Buyers scan it and browse your whole stock with live market prices while you keep dealing. No more flipping through binders or answering "how much?" for every card.

## For your buyers

- They see your full stock on their phone, with photos, condition, live prices and 90 days of price history
- They build a list and send it to you with one tap. No app and no account needed
- They get an emailed receipt after the sale

## For you

- **Live market pricing:** cards are priced as you add them and re-priced every night. Set your own rule (e.g. 90% of market) and rounding
- **Fast setup:** search the catalog to add a card, or import a CSV
- **Instant alerts:** a ping and push notification as soon as a buyer sends a list. Stock is held for them so it can't be sold twice
- **See your table:** how many people are on your store page right now, and every list being built, each tagged so you can match it to a buyer
- **Quick sale:** tap "Sell" for walk-up buyers, no email needed
- **Saved receipts:** cash, card or PayPal is recorded on every sale
- **Ledger and reports:** takings, cost, profit, stock value, every stock change, and CSV export
- **Open, pause or close** your store at any time, and preview it exactly as buyers see it
- **Your branding:** your own banner, profile image and store link
