# tcgmart.app: run your card business, online and at shows

One live-priced store for your Pokemon stock. Share the link online, or put the QR on your show table. Buyers browse on their phone, fill a cart and send it to you. You confirm the sale.

## For your buyers

- Your full stock on their phone, with photos, condition, live prices and 90 days of price history
- They fill a cart and send it with one tap. No app and no account needed
- An emailed receipt after the sale

## For you

- **Live market pricing:** cards are priced as you add them and re-priced every night. Set your own rule (e.g. 90% of market) and rounding
- **Fast setup:** search the catalog by name, set or number, or import a CSV
- **Sell online or at a table:** one setting switches your store's wording and holds for either way of selling
- **Instant alerts:** a ping and push notification as soon as a cart comes in. Stock is held for the buyer so it can't be sold twice
- **See who's browsing:** how many people are on your store now, and every cart being built
- **Quick sale:** tap "Sell" for walk-up buyers at a show, no email needed
- **Buys and trade-ins:** record cards you buy; the price paid becomes your cost
- **Ledger and reports:** takings, cost, profit, stock value, every stock change, voids, a cash check at close, and CSV exports
- **Open, pause or close** your store at any time, and preview it exactly as buyers see it
- **Your branding:** your own banner, profile image and store link at tcgmart.app
